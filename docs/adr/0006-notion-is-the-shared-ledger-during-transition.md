# Notion is the shared ledger while two workflows coexist

For a while, applications are prepared both by ApplAI and by the author's older Claude Code workflow, which already records everything in a Notion database. To avoid applying twice to the same offer, Notion stays the single ledger during that period: ApplAI reads the rows the old workflow creates and writes after each of its own sends, keyed by the offer's official URL. Once ApplAI covers every ATS in use, it becomes the source of truth, the sync turns one-way, then stops.
